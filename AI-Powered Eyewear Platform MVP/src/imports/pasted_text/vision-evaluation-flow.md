Continue working on the existing FRAME MVP. Do not redesign the existing visual language or replace the current aesthetic personalization flow.

We now need to add a MEDICAL / VISION EVALUATION layer to the product.

IMPORTANT:

* The interface language must remain RUSSIAN.
* Keep all existing visual styling, colors, typography, spacing, glassmorphism and premium eyewear aesthetic.
* The medical functionality should feel like a natural extension of the existing product, not like a separate hospital or clinical dashboard.
* This is still an MVP concept for internal presentation.
* Do not claim that the prototype can make a medical diagnosis.
* Use wording such as "оценка зрения", "предварительная оценка", "параметры зрения", "рекомендации", and "результаты проверки".
* Clearly communicate that a professional examination may still be required.
* The purpose of the medical layer is to demonstrate how FRAME could eventually connect aesthetic personalization with vision-related personalization.

## NEW CORE PRODUCT CONCEPT

The product is no longer only:

"Find glasses that look good on you."

It becomes:

"Find glasses that suit both YOU and YOUR VISION."

The new user journey should be:

PHOTO
↓
FACE & STYLE ANALYSIS
↓
VISION EVALUATION
↓
PERSONAL VISION PROFILE
↓
COMBINED RECOMMENDATION
↓
VIRTUAL TRY-ON
↓
GLASSES CUSTOMIZATION
↓
FINAL PERSONALIZED DESIGN

The key idea:

Aesthetic data tells us:
"What looks good on you?"

Vision data tells us:
"What may be appropriate for your visual needs?"

Together:

"Which eyewear is the best match for you?"
