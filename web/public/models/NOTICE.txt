polyhaven/round-spectacles.glb
Round Spectacles by Sean Buckley
License: CC0 1.0 https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
https://polyhaven.com/a/round_spectacles
Fetched 2026-10-20 from the published 1k glTF export and packed into one GLB
with @gltf-transform/cli cp. Geometry is otherwise byte-faithful to upstream:
7822 uploaded vertices, two materials, frame and glass kept separate, front
width 157.8 mm as authored in metres upstream. The metre to millimetre scale is
applied at load time by fitting the model's measured front width to the
catalogue size code, so the shipped file can be re-downloaded and compared
against upstream.

These meshes are the open FaceFilter VTO sample (not Jeeliz GlassesDB / commercial widget).

No third-party branded eyewear is shipped or imitated. Ray-Ban, Prada, Oakley,
Moscot and similar designs exist as CC-BY uploads on model marketplaces; copying
a protected silhouette and naming it is trademark exposure, so every other
catalogue model is generated from its own size code by src/lib/glasses-rig.ts.
